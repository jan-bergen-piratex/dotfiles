## Best Question Set

1. Which recurring manual work do you do that feels like list checking, sorting, copying, or researching?
2. Where do you already have strong judgment rules in your head?
3. For OMClub acquisition: what makes a company a good sponsor, barter partner, guest lead, or bad fit?
4. Which sources do you use: spreadsheets, Gmail, HubSpot, guest lists, event websites, Slack, Google Drive?
5. What are the categories you naturally sort people/companies into?
6. Which mistakes would be embarrassing if an assistant made them?
7. What output would actually help you: table, draft message, CRM update, shortlist, review queue?
8. What must stay human-approved?
9. What examples can you give me: 5 good, 5 bad, 5 borderline?
10. If we automated one painful process first, which one saves most time or creates most revenue?
